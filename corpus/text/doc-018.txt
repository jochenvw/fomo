Agreement
This Offshore Catering and Coffee Supply Agreement is made between Contoso Oil PLC
("Contoso") and Fourth Coffee Offshore Ltd ("Supplier") following what the operations team still
calls the Great Offshore Coffee Supply Debacle of 2025.
Supplier shall provide catering, galley staff and an uninterrupted supply of coffee to platforms
Alpha, Bravo and Delta.
1. Service Levels
1.1 Supplier shall maintain a minimum of fourteen (14) days of coffee stock on each platform at all
times.
1.2 A coffee stock-out exceeding twelve (12) hours is a Service Failure.
2. Charges and Payment
2.1 Charges are GBP 38.50 per person on board per day.
2.2 Contoso shall pay each valid monthly invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt.
2.3 Each Service Failure entitles Contoso to a service credit of GBP 2,500, deducted from the next
invoice.
3. Term and Termination
3.1 The term is two (2) years from 1 May 2026.
3.2 Contoso may terminate on thirty (30) days' notice after three Service Failures in any rolling
six-month period.
