Procurement Policy PP-07: Supplier Payment
 Terms
FICTIONAL - synthetic workshop material
Purpose and Scope
This policy applies to all Contoso Oil employees who negotiate, approve or administer supplier
agreements. It defines default payment terms and approval thresholds.
Policy Requirements
R1. The default payment term for new supplier contracts is thirty (30) days from receipt of a valid
invoice.
R2. Payment terms longer than sixty (60) days require CFO approval.
R3. Small and medium suppliers must be offered terms no longer than thirty (30) days.
R4. Early settlement discounts may be accepted only where Treasury confirms liquidity.
Model Clause (for reference only)
Contract templates should use: "The Company shall pay each correct invoice within thirty (30) days
of receipt." This model clause is guidance and does not itself create obligations with any supplier.
Responsibilities
Category managers own compliance. Internal Audit reviews a sample of agreements yearly.
Policy owner: Head of Procurement. Effective date: 1 January 2026.
