Framework Agreement
This Framework Agreement is made between Contoso Oil PLC ("Client") and Proseware Integrity
Services Inc. ("Supplier") for in-line inspection (ILI), external corrosion direct assessment and
reporting on the Contoso export pipeline system.
1. Call-off Orders
1.1 Services are requested through call-off orders that reference this Framework Agreement. Each
call-off order states the pipeline segment, tool type and schedule.
2. Fees and Payment
2.1 Fees are payable against milestones: forty percent (40%) on tool run completion, forty percent
(40%) on delivery of the preliminary report and twenty percent (20%) on acceptance of the final
report.
2.2 Client shall pay undisputed milestone invoices within thirty (30) days of receipt.
3. Liability
3.1 Supplier's aggregate liability under any call-off order is capped at one hundred percent (100%)
of the call-off order value.
4. Term
4.1 This Framework Agreement runs for four (4) years from 1 April 2026.
