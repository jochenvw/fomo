Master Purchase Terms
These Master Purchase Terms (the "Terms") are agreed between Contoso Oil PLC ("Buyer") and
Litware Drilling Supplies BV ("Seller") and govern every purchase order for drilling consumables,
including drill bits, casing centralisers and drilling fluid additives.
1. Orders
1.1 Each purchase order issued by Buyer and acknowledged by Seller forms a binding commitment
on these Terms.
1.2 Seller shall deliver DAP Aberdeen supply base within ten (10) working days.
2. Price and Settlement
2.1 Prices are fixed for calendar year 2026 per the attached price schedule.
2.2 Buyer shall settle each correct invoice sixty (60) days end of month.
2.3 An early settlement discount of two percent (2%) applies where Buyer pays within ten (10) days
of invoice.
3. Warranty
3.1 Seller warrants that goods conform to API specifications and are free of defects for twelve (12)
months from delivery.
4. Duration
4.1 These Terms apply from 1 February 2026 until 31 January 2028 and may be ended by either
side with thirty (30) days' notice in writing.
