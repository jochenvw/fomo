Agreement
This Crew Transfer Services Agreement is made between Contoso Oil PLC ("Customer") and
Tailspin Aviation Ltd ("Operator") for scheduled helicopter crew changes between Aberdeen Dyce
and the Bravo and Delta platforms.
1. Services
1.1 Operator shall provide two AW169 aircraft with crews and a guaranteed availability of
ninety-seven percent (97%) per calendar month.
2. Price and Payment Terms
2.1 A standing charge of GBP 410,000 per month applies, plus GBP 3,150 per flight hour.
2.2 A fuel surcharge is adjusted quarterly against the published Jet A-1 index.
2.3 Customer shall pay each correct invoice within thirty (30) days of the invoice date.
3. Term
3.1 The Agreement commences on 1 June 2026 for five (5) years. Customer may terminate for
convenience on one hundred and eighty (180) days' notice.
