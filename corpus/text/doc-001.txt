Parties
This Time Charter Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Contoso Oil PLC, Harbour
House, Aberdeen (the "Charterer") and Northwind Marine Services AS, Stavanger (the "Owner"),
each a "Party".
The Owner agrees to let and the Charterer agrees to hire the platform supply vessel MV Northwind
Petrel (IMO 9900014) for supply runs to the Bravo and Delta platforms.
1. Term
1.1 The charter period is twenty-four (24) months from the delivery date of 1 March 2026, with two
optional extension periods of six (6) months each.
1.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement on ninety (90) days' written notice after the first
twelve months.
2. Hire and Payment Terms
2.1 The day rate is EUR 18,450 per day, payable monthly in arrears.
2.2 Payment terms: the Charterer shall pay each correct invoice within forty-five (45) days of receipt
of invoice.
2.3 Late payment interest of 1.5% per month shall accrue on undisputed amounts unpaid after the
due date.
2.4 Fuel, lubricants and port dues are for the Charterer's account at cost, without mark-up.
3. Off-hire
3.1 No hire is payable for any period exceeding six (6) consecutive hours during which the vessel is
unable to perform due to breakdown or crew deficiency.
4. Liability and Insurance
4.1 Each Party shall be responsible for injury to its own personnel and loss of its own property,
regardless of fault (knock-for-knock).
4.2 The Owner shall maintain hull and machinery and P&I insurance for the term.
5. Governing Law
5.1 This Agreement is governed by English law. Disputes shall be referred to arbitration in London.
