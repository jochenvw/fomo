Agreement
This Rig Maintenance Services Agreement is entered into by Contoso Oil PLC ("Company") and
Fabrikam Rig Services Ltd ("Contractor") on 15 January 2026.
Contractor shall provide planned and corrective maintenance for drilling rigs Contoso-12 and
Contoso-15, including top drives, mud pumps and BOP control systems.
1. Scope and Service Levels
1.1 Contractor shall respond to critical breakdowns within four (4) hours and restore operation
within twenty-four (24) hours where spare parts are available on site.
1.2 Planned maintenance follows the schedule in Appendix A.
2. Compensation and Payment
2.1 Company shall pay the monthly service fee of GBP 212,000 plus approved call-out charges at
the rates in Appendix B.
2.2 Invoices are issued monthly in arrears; payment is due thirty (30) days after the end of the
month in which a valid invoice is received.
2.3 Company shall withhold a retention of five percent (5%) of each payment, released upon
acceptance of the annual maintenance audit.
3. Term and Termination
3.1 This Agreement remains in force for three (3) years.
3.2 Company may terminate for convenience on sixty (60) days' written notice. Either party may
terminate immediately for material breach not remedied within fourteen (14) days.
4. HSE
4.1 Contractor personnel shall comply with Company's Permit to Work policy and Life Saving Rules.
